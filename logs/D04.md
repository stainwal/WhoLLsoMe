## 3. Flags

- **Calibration is rough.** The Usage bar moves in 1% steps, and the pilot moved weekly by under 1% per run. Working estimate: about 1.4% of the week per 100,000 words per extraction pass (±50%), assuming session % ≈ 5.5 × weekly %. Sonnet and Opus are indistinguishable at this resolution. An earlier estimate of "2–3 weeks for the full corpus" was wrong (it ignored caching) and was corrected in-session.
- **SuttaCentral holds more than the policy lists:** Sutta Nipata, Udana, Itivuttaka, Theragatha, Therigatha, Jataka and others. All are CC0; all were skipped. Added to the parking list.
- **Rumi:** couplet and heading boundaries were inferred from a scan. About 15 verse lines were misclassified as headings (marked `meta.heuristic`). Worth a look before Rumi is extracted.
- **Meditations:** the source has no standard section numbers, so locations read "Book 2, paragraph 3", not "2.3".
- **Gutenberg italics** (`_word_`) are kept in the text. Strip them for display in the app.
- **Zitkala-Ša:** the quoted municipal report in "America's Indian Problem" carries `meta.quoted_report`. Exclude it at extraction.
- **Near-duplicates in the pool:** some passages appear twice because the models ended them at different sentences (for example, two versions of one Vivekananda passage). The Day 5 review should pick one ending.
- **Effort level was not varied.** Whether higher effort changes selectivity is untested.
- **Week-start state:** about 55% of the weekly limit was left unused before the Thursday 05:30 IST reset. Tonight's work used about 3 points in total.
