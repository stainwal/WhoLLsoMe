## 3. Flags

- **Noise.** Golden gaps of 2–4 statements are 3–7 points; the two finalists differ from each other by less than that. The consistent signal is the drop in first passages graded 0 (golden 11 → 5–6; human held-out 4 → 1–2).
- **Rewrite results are a ceiling.** Opus wrote the rewrites offline; at runtime it would be Haiku or Sonnet, adding a model call (cost, latency, a place for user text to go). Experiment 5 on Day 20 decides.
- **Judge leniency differs by model.** Opus is stricter on tone than Fable; the top-1-graded-0 metric partly measures the Opus judge's sensitivity. Opus stays the judge of record, as decided.
- **The H03 disagreement** points to a prompt gap: the judge rewards passages that mirror the problem without offering anything. Not changed mid-experiment (it would invalidate comparisons); a candidate for judge prompt v2 before the Day 21 gate.
- **Experiments 1 and 2 had one candidate model** (`voyage-4`) instead of 2–3. The larger model didn't help, so others weren't tried.
- The pre-commit hook is the only guard on push; nothing new was pushed after `6b8df0b`.
